`NoEntryTape.png` is an original asset generated with the built-in image generation tool. No stock-photo watermark was removed. Final prompt: “Create an original game texture asset: two yellow safety tape strips crossing in a broad X, like a facility access barrier. Each strip repeatedly reads exactly 'NO ENTRY' in large bold black condensed industrial lettering. Straight clean tape edges, slight realistic vinyl creases, bright safety yellow. Front orthographic view, entire X contained with margin. The four spaces around the strips and outside the X must be genuinely transparent. No wall, door, stock-photo marks, logos, border, or extra words. This is an original asset inspired by a common safety tape motif, not an edit of a stock image. Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: transparent Unity door decal.”
